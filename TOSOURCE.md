## PART 3 — Lettrism & Hurufiyya (Secondary Priority)

### §F — Ibn Arabi, Futahat al-Makkiyya (Letter Diagrams)

The Gardiner volume (in your PDF collection) includes a chapter by Ali Karjoo-Ravary
on Ibn Arabi's letter-form diagrams in *al-Futahat al-Makkiyya* (Meccan Openings).
These are geometric diagrams illustrating each letter's cosmic form.

**Key MSS:**
- Konya MS (Turkey, Mevlana Museum) — best illustrated copy, 13th c.
- BnF Ar. 2659 — another Paris copy
- Bodleian MS Hunt 505

**Accessible via:**
- Bodleian: https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk → search "Futuhat"

## PART 5 — Access Instructions

### §A — JTS Digital Collections (Manuscripts)

**URL:** https://makor.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/collectionDiscovery?vid=01JTS_INST:01JTS&lang=en

1. Click "Manuscript Collection"
2. Search for: "Sefer Yetzirah" or specific shelfmarks (JTS 1609, JTS 1584, JTS 1895, JTS 1884)
   - JTS uses numeric call numbers; try searching "ENA 1609" or just "1609"
3. When you find the digitized manuscript, download individual folio images
4. JTS images are typically free for research; note any license shown

**Alternative:** The JTS manuscripts may be in HathiTrust or the IMHM (Jewish Manuscript
Preservation Committee) database at the National Library of Israel:
https://web.nli.org.il/sites/nlis/en/manuscript

### §B — Biblioteca Palatina, Parma (MS Parma 1390, MS Parma 2784)

**URL:** https://bibliotecapalatina.beniculturali.it

The Parma kabbalah MSS are partially digitized via:
- **Europeana:** https://www.europeana.eu → search "Parma 1390 kabbalah"
- **IMHM (NLI):** The Institute of Microfilmed Hebrew Manuscripts at the National
  Library of Israel holds microfilms of most Hebrew MSS worldwide, including Parma.
  https://web.nli.org.il → Manuscripts → IMHM
